## What

In test, users are tested on all the information of the system at once. The questions are mixed, tailored to the individual learner: If you have been struggling with a specific chapter, it will ask more about that.

## How it works

1. The learner starts the test from the Test page, which also shows the test log with previous tests.
2. The test has six questions by default. A question can be a task to carry out in the interface, a scenario to watch and describe, or a question to answer.
3. The [test toolbar](/documentation/components/test-toolbar) along the bottom moves between the questions. In a scenario, it floats over the screen and the [toast](/documentation/components/toast) says the task.
4. The results list every question with a score, and end with the total. The test is added to the training log.

## Why

Testing boosts retention, and retrieving an answer, or producing an action yourself, lasts longer than being shown it <SourcePill href="/research#roediger-karpicke-testing">Roediger & Karpicke (2006)</SourcePill>. Mixing questions from several chapters is a harder and more effective interleaved practice <SourcePill href="/research#bjork-bjork-desirable-difficulties">Bjork & Bjork (2011)</SourcePill>, than just testing at the end of every chapter, like in "learn". The test of a chapter right after the walkthrough only tests once, and the value shows up when it is repeated later, which is what the mixed test is for.
