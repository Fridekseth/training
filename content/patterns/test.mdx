## What

The test is a test of all the chapters at once. The questions are mixed, and more of them come from the chapters where the learner's results are lowest. Each chapter also ends in a test of its own.

## How it works

1. The learner starts the test from the Test page, which says which chapters most of the questions will come from, and shows the training log and the last test.
2. The test has six questions by default. A question is a task to carry out on the screen, a scenario to watch and describe, or a question to answer.
3. A chapter gets more questions the further its score is from full. A chapter that has not been taken counts as a middling one, and two questions in a row do not come from the same chapter while others have some left.
4. The [test toolbar](/documentation/components/test-toolbar) along the bottom moves between the questions. In a scenario, it floats over the screen and the [toast](/documentation/components/toast) says the task.
5. The results list every question with a score, and end with the total. The test is added to the training log.

## Why

Testing boosts retention, and retrieving an answer, or producing an action yourself, lasts longer than being shown it <SourcePill href="/research#roediger-karpicke-testing">Roediger & Karpicke (2006)</SourcePill>.

Mixing questions from several chapters is the harder and more effective interleaved version <SourcePill href="/research#bjork-bjork-desirable-difficulties">Bjork & Bjork (2011)</SourcePill>. The test of a chapter right after the walkthrough only tests once, and the value shows up when it is repeated later, which is what the mixed test is for.
