## What

The overview is the home of the training, and meant to give the user a personal view of their training and progress. It gives personalised recommendations for learning activities, and a log with previous training and results.

## How it works

- Overview is the first page in the training menu.
- The page greets the learner with two personalised recommendations for training, linking directly to the activities.
- Under the cards is the training log: a list of finished chapters, tests and scenarios, with the date, the result and the progress.

## Why

The personalised recommendations nudge the learner to practice based on the time since the last practice. That is supported by the spacing effect <SourcePill href="/research#ebbinghaus-memory">Ebbinghaus (1885)</SourcePill>, and the right gap depends on how long the knowledge has to last <SourcePill href="/research#carpenter-spacing">Carpenter et al. (2012)</SourcePill>.
