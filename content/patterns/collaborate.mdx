## What

In collaborate mode the learner can add comments for their team, and read others' tips and tricks. It is the same screen as explore mode, with the comments tool on from the start.

## How it works

1. The learner starts collaborate mode from the Collaborate page, which also lists the latest comments to read and open.
2. Comments sit on the components as [comment pins](/documentation/components/comment-pin). Unread ones are drawn dark, and read ones light.
3. A pin opens a [thread](/documentation/components/thread) at the side of the screen. While it is open the other pins are disabled, and the part of the screen it is about is left clear.
4. The first message of a thread is pinned to the top. A reply is written in the field at the bottom, with the on-screen keyboard.

## Why

Captains can share experiences even though they do not work together. This was one of the three ideas to test for the Wavefoil case.

The comments are the part of the pattern with the least support in the sources collected so far. Nothing read covers learning from peers, so literature on social learning is still to be found.
