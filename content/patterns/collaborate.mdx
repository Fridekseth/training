## What

Collaborate is all about facilitating for collaboration and communication on shift based workplaces. Users can add comments for their team, and read comments from others. It can be used to share experiences between shifts, or between people working in different places.

## How it works

1. The learner starts collaborate mode from the Collaborate page, which also lists the latest comments to read and open.
2. Comments sit on the components as [comment pins](/documentation/components/comment-pin). Unread ones are drawn dark, and read ones light.
3. A pin opens a [thread](/documentation/components/thread) at the side of the screen. While it is open the other pins are disabled, and the component in question is highlighted.
4. Messages can be pinned to the top.

## Why

Industrial workplaces are often shift based, meaning there are often multiple people with knowledge and experience of the systems that do not necessarily speak to each other that often. With the collaborate tool, users can share experiences and learn from each other even though they do not work together.
