## What

The second screen takes learning out of the bridge, by providing learning content suited for phones or tablets.

## How it works

This pattern is at an early stage. The first prototype is a second screen for a phone, that tests out different ways of adding more interesting interactions to learning. I have tested three different concepts:

- Learning the pitch, roll and heave instrument, by using the phone's gyro to simulate the movements on the phone. The instrument is triggered by the movements of the phone itself: tipping the phone forward or back changes the pitch and so on.
- Exploring how components change over time, by changing the parameters. In the example the user can change the wave conditions and see how the graph changes.
- Digital handover. Making handovers between shifts available on the phone, allowing for smoother communication between crew.

## Why

It is not always practical to do the training at work, and the time in between, such as the commute or the time spent in the cabin at sea, could be used for learning. Users I've talked to have pointed out they would like to learn, and stay updated at home. At the [OpenBridge seminar](/blog/openbridge-seminar-feedback), the iPad as a second screen got a good response in both sessions, and a participant described staff who trained on tablets during their commute.
