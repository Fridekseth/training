## What

The second screen takes learning out of the bridge. It is not always practical to do the training at work, and the time in between, such as the commute on land or the time in the cabin at sea, could be used for learning.

## How it works

This pattern is at an early stage. The first prototype is a second screen for a phone, with the vessel's pitch, roll and heave instrument inside the outline of a hull. The instrument is moved by the phone itself: tipping the phone forward or back changes the pitch, tipping it side to side changes the roll, and lifting it up and down changes the heave.

## Why

The Wavefoil operator pointed out that the training does not have to be only in the bridge screen, and could imagine using it at home. At the [OpenBridge seminar](/blog/openbridge-seminar-feedback), the iPad as a second screen got a good response in both sessions, and a participant described staff who trained on tablets during their commute.
