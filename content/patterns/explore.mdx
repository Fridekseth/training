## What

In explore mode the learner can click around in the interface without controlling the system. It is a safe place to try the application. It is only available when the system is not in use.

## How it works

1. The learner starts explore mode from the Explore page.
2. The screen is framed in teal, so it is clear that it is not the live system.
3. The [mode tab](/documentation/components/mode-tab) in the corner opens a menu with two tools: Look around and Comments.
4. With Look around, the screen shows the hand [cursor](/documentation/components/cursors), and everything can be pressed without any effect on the system.
5. With Comments, the learner can place a [comment pin](/documentation/components/comment-pin) on a component, or open the ones that others have left.

## Why

A system with real consequences can be learned by trying things out, if the practice space is clearly separate <SourcePill href="/research#stripe-sandbox">Stripe</SourcePill>. Crew are often afraid to make mistakes at first, and the person from Wavefoil said a big part of introducing the foils is to make them feel safe.

The teal frame shows the user that they are in a different mode, as the green dev mode in Figma does <SourcePill href="/research#figma-onboarding">Figma</SourcePill>.
