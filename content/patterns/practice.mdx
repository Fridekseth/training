## What

Practice is for critical situations, in simulated scenarios. It is recommended to practice scenarios regularly, in order to keep the routines for when a critical situation happens.

## How it works

1. The learner chooses a scenario from the Practice page, which lists them with a result, a status and a button to start or open them.
2. The ship screen is framed in teal like the other modes, and then something happens on it that the learner has to deal with.
3. The [toast](/documentation/components/toast) at the top says what the task is.
4. The steps the learner should take are listed in the menu from the [mode tab](/documentation/components/mode-tab).
5. Nothing the learner does reaches the real system.

## Why

The recommended practice on the [overview](/documentation/patterns/overview) comes when there has not been a critical alarm for a long time, so the routines are kept in use.
