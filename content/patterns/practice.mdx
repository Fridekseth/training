## What

Practice lets the user get familiar with critical situations and edge cases through simulated scenarios. The idea is that if scenarios are practiced regularly, users will keep the routines for when a critical situation happens.

## How it works

1. The learner chooses a scenario from the Practice page, which lists them with a result, a status and a button to start or open them.
2. The screen is framed in teal like the other modes. Once the scenario starts, the interface will simulate it by for example triggering an alarm.
3. The learner has to deal with the scenario in the correct way. The steps the learner should take are listed in the menu from the [mode tab](/documentation/components/mode-tab).
4. Nothing the learner does reaches the real system.
5. When the scenario is completed, the learner receives a score based on how they handled the scenario.

## Why

Carrying out an action yourself lasts longer than being shown it <SourcePill href="/research#roediger-karpicke-testing">Roediger & Karpicke (2006)</SourcePill> <SourcePill href="/research#bjork-bjork-desirable-difficulties">Bjork & Bjork (2011)</SourcePill>, and with the recommended practice on the [overview](/documentation/patterns/overview) the routines are kept in use.
