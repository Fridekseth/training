## What

Styles for elevation and hierarchy in learning are used to guide attention in the interface.

- **Overlay:** a transparent teal wash over the interface, dimming what is not in focus, without hiding it.
- **Focus:** The part of the screen in focus is marked with a teal outline.
- **Highlight:** Overlay and focus combined can highlight specific part of the interface.
- **Hover:** When overlay is not in use, a teal overlay can be placed on

## Why

When adding a new layer on top of already complex interfaces, it can quickly become cluttered and messy. Overlays and outlines help create elevation and focus, so the user knows where to look.

## How to use

There are four styles of hierarchy, overlay, focus, highlight and hover. Only lead attention to one element at a time, and use the styles sparingly, to preserve the effect.

In addition to these, learning uses the elevation styles from OpenBridge.
