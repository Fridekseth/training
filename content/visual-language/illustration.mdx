## Why

The use of illustration in the UI can shift the tone of communication to something more human and approachable, bringing personality and humor to an otherwise sterile environment. At the same time, the strict style of the illustrations, with an isometric perspective and geometric shape, makes them ideal for recreation, and an illustration language fitting for the OpenBridge library.

## How to use

Illustrations can be used to aid communication, where there is room for it. They should only be used if there are sufficient space for them.

Illustration guidelines:

- Should be drawn over an isometric grid
- Use 2px stroke
- Rounded outer corners
- Size: about 140x140 px, depending on the figure.
- Use shadow on the right side, where it would be natural on the figure.
