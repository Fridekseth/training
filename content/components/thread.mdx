## Overview

The thread is the panel that opens at the side of the screen when a [comment pin](/documentation/components/comment-pin) is pressed. It holds the conversation about one part of the screen, and a field to write an answer in. It is used in Explore and Collaborate.

## Features

- Has a header with a comment icon, what the thread is about, and a button to close it.
- Pins the first message at the top of the thread, with a badge that says so.
- Shows the learner's own messages on the right, with filled initials, and everyone else's on the left, with outlined ones.
- Scrolls to the newest message when one is added or another thread is opened.
- Has a field at the bottom with a toolbar, voice recording and a send button. Pressing the field opens the on-screen keyboard, so the system keyboard never covers the screen.
- Stands on the left or the right of the screen, and can start below the top bar.

## Variants

- **Side.** On the left or the right of the screen.
- **Below the top bar.** It starts 48 px from the top instead of at the top.
- **Subject.** The header has what the thread is about, or the title of the thread when it has no subject.
