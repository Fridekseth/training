## Overview

The step menu is the list of the steps in a chapter. It is opened from the [mode tab](/documentation/components/mode-tab), and it has a way out of training mode at the bottom.

## Features

- Has the title and icon of the chapter on top, and a card for each step, with its number and a dot on a line.
- Marks how far the learner has come: the line and the dots are teal up to the current step and grey after it.
- Goes to a step when its card is pressed.
- Lists every step from the current one on. When the chapter is longer than the menu has room for, the steps nearest the end are packed into a stack in front of the last one, with a dotted line where they were.
- Leaves off the oldest steps at the top only when there is nothing left to pack.
- Sits at the side of the corner the tab is in, clear of the tab.
- Ends with the button Exit training mode.

## Variants

- **Whole chapter.** Every step has its own card, when the chapter fits.
- **Packed.** The last steps are a stack, when it does not fit.
- **Corner.** The menu sits on the left or the right, and at the top or the bottom, with the tab.
