## Overview

The step menu is used to navigate in a sequence with several steps. It is opened from the [mode tab](/documentation/components/mode-tab), and it has a way out of training mode at the bottom. It is built using patterns from the sequence components in OpenBridge.

## Features

- Has the title and icon of the chapter or sequence you are in on top, and a card for each step, with its number and a progress line.
- Marks how far the learner has come: the line and the dots are filled up to the current step and grey after it.
- Navigates to a step when its card is pressed.
- Lists every step from the current one on. When the chapter is longer than the menu has room for, the steps nearest the end are packed into a stack.
- Sits at the side of the corner the tab is in, clear of the tab.
- Ends with the button Exit training mode.

## Variants

- **Whole chapter.** Every step has its own card, when the chapter fits.
- **Packed.** The last steps are a stack, when it does not fit.
- **Corner.** The menu sits on the left or the right, and at the top or the bottom, with the tab.
