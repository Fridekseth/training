## Overview

The rich button is a custom version of the OpenBridge rich button. It is a card that leads to a page of the training, such as the recommended practice on the overview page. It has a drawing on a teal plate in place of the icon, and a badge for something that needs attention.

## Features

- Has a label in bold, a line of text under it, and a chevron on the right.
- Has a drawing on a teal plate in front. The plate is left out when there is no drawing.
- Can have a badge in the top right corner, for something that needs attention.
- Changes colour when the pointer is over it, and again when it is pressed.
- Gets an outline when it is reached with the keyboard.
- Can be disabled.

## Variants

- **With or without a drawing.** The plate is only there when there is a drawing.
- **With or without a badge.**
- **Enabled or disabled.**
