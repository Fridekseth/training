## Overview

The guided step is an explanation box that points at one part of the screen, and it is how Learn explains a screen. The rest of the screen is washed, so the part that is explained stands out, and there is a way to go back and on.

## Features

- Has a teal header with the icon of training and the name of the step, and a body with the explanation.
- Has Previous and Next. Previous is disabled on the first step, and the last step says Finish instead of Next.
- Points at the part it explains, with a pointer on the side of the box that faces it, centred on that part.
- Glides from one step to the next, since every box moves on the same properties.
- Can lead on to a test, when the label of the button that goes on says so.

## Variants

- **Pointer side.** The pointer is on the left, right, top or bottom of the box, or the box has none when it explains the screen as a whole.
- **Placed from the top or the bottom.** A step that explains something above the box hangs the box from the bottom of the screen.
- **Narrow.** A box narrower than 300 px has less space between the icon and the words on its buttons.
