## Overview

The guided step is an explanation box that points at one part of the screen. It can have both text, image and interactions. It is based on the OpenBridge Toggletip, so it follows familiar styling, but used in a new context.

## Features

- Has a teal header with an icon and title.
- Has a content box for text, images or other content.
- Has action buttons on the bottom.
- Points at what it explains, with a pointer on the side of the box.
- Gliding animation from one step to the next, when multiple boxes are used in a row.

## Variants

- **Pointer side.** The pointer is on the left, right, top or bottom of the box, or the box has none when it explains the screen as a whole.
