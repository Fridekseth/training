## Overview

The training frame is the teal frame around the screen, that shows up whenever the system is in learning mode. It is there in every mode, from the moment training mode starts until it is left.

## Features

- Is drawn once around the screen when training mode starts, from the bottom right corner.
- Is a plain line once it has been drawn, with no seam where the drawing ended.
- Always shows up together with the mode tab.

## Variants

The frame has one form. It is the same in every mode.
