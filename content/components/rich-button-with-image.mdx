## Overview

The rich button with image is a custom version of the OpenBridge rich button, but with the added possibility to add an image or illustration instead of a leading icon.

## Features

- Has a label in bold, a line of text under it, and a trailing icon, that could be a chevron for example.
- Has a slot for an image or illustration.
- Can have a badge in the top right corner, for something that needs attention.
- Has enabled, hover, active, focused and disabled states.

## Variants

- With or without an image.
- With or without a badge.
- Enabled, hover, active, focused and disabled states.
