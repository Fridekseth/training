## Overview

The comment pin marks a comment on the screen. Pressing it opens the thread that belongs to it. It is used in Explore and Collaborate.

## Features

- Is drawn dark when the comment is unread, and light when it has been read.
- Opens into the initials of the people who have written in it when its comment is open.
- Disables the other pins while one comment is open.
- Is pushed back inside the screen when it is near an edge, so the open pin is never cut off.

## Variants

- **Unread and read.** Dark and light.
- **Enabled, checked and disabled.** Enabled is a pin that can be pressed, checked is the one that is open, and disabled is the others while that one is open.
