## Overview

The mode intro shows up in the middle of the screen as a mode is started, informing of the mode that has been opened. After a moment it settles into the corner and turns into the [mode tab](/documentation/components/mode-tab).

## Features

- Names the mode with a large icon and a title, and a second line under it when there is something more to say.
- Stays in the middle for about two seconds, and then moves to the corner the tab is in.
- Shrinks its icon and its words as it moves, and ends up as the tab, with the words the tab has.

## Variants

- **With a subtitle.** The title has a second line under it. Used when the mode is about something, such as a chapter.
- **Title only.** The title stands alone, in the middle of the box.
