## Overview

The toast is the bar that says what the learner is to do while a scenario runs, such as Deploy the foils. It has a button to cancel the scenario, with a ring round it that shows how the scenario is going.

## Features

- Names the task in bold, with a second line under it when there is more to say.
- Has a cancel button with a ring round it. The ring turns while the sequence starts, and runs down while the time to do the task goes.
- Has a handle at the front, with a line after it, to move the bar off whatever it covers. The arrow keys move it too.
- Stays inside the screen when it is moved.
- Can have an icon in front of the title in place of the handle, for a toast that is not moved.

## Variants

- **Ring.** Turning while the sequence starts, or running down with the time that is left.
- **Movable.** With a handle, or with an icon.
- **Title only, or title and subtitle.** A scenario has only a title.
