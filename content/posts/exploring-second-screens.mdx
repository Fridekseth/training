After the talks with industry professionals at the OpenBridge Seminar, and the feedback from the Wavefoil operator, I have decided to further explore the potential in second screens. It is not always practical to do the training at work, and the in-between time, such as commuting, or the time spent in the cabin at sea, could be utilized for productive learning.

I've created a few concepts to test out, designed for mobile. I wanted to utilize the potential in the phone interactions that desktop doesn't have, like touch, haptics and gyro:

### Component documentation gyro

{/* Add photos or a video of the gyro concept here. */}

One of the concepts in the maritime field that I've had the most trouble learning is the motion of the vessel in the water. This inspired me to use the gyro sensors in the phone to make a more tactile experience. The idea is that the phone represents the vessel, and that you can tip it in different directions and see how the movements register in the instruments.

### Component documentation touch and haptics

{/* Add photos or a video of the wave conditions concept here. */}

With a touch screen, more playful interactions are much more accessible. For this Wave conditions component, I've tested making the documentation interactive, so you can change the wave parameter and watch it change.

Playful interactions like the two above, might be effective in making the initial training and onboarding more engaging.

### Digital handover

{/* Add photos or a video of the handover concept here. */}

To further build on the «collaborate» idea, I've explored how the comments can be tied to the handover during changing of crew. What if you could get a full overview of everything new since your last shift? This would solve some of the moderating challenges that were raised at the seminar, and give the comments a direct recipient and purpose rather than just floating around in the system somewhere.

### Next steps

I've prepared these concepts, both as digital interactive prototypes and paper versions, to take with me on the upcoming field trip to a ship later this month. There I plan to get user feedback on the concepts, and to further map out these «in-between» moments on a ship, to get a better understanding of where and when there is potential for second screen learning.
