So far the project has run on two tracks. On one I have been reading about how people learn, in
[the first round of source searching](/blog/grunnleggende-laeringsprinsipper) and in
[the look at training inside interfaces](/blog/opplaering-i-grensesnittet). On the other I have been
building the [Wavefoil prototype](/blog/introducing-training-in-wavefoil). This post puts the two side by
side: what in the prototype has support in the theory, and where does the theory suggest I have got it wrong
or left something out.

## A gap in the sources

![Explore mode with a comment thread open on the left, next to the component it is about](/posts/connecting-prototype-to-learning-theory/explore-thread.png)
*A comment thread in Explore mode.*

The comments that captains can leave for each other have nothing behind them in what I have collected.
Vervaeke touches on emotion and context, but nothing I have read covers learning from peers. If comments
stay a central part of the concept, I will need to look for literature on social learning.

## What this suggests trying next

Reading the two tracks together points at a few areas of improvement: testing and comments. Repeating the test later and mixing questions
from different chapters could improve the effect of the testing. To further develop the comment function, it could be beneficial to look into research on the topic, and learn from similar concepts.
