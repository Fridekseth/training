# About this project

This project explores how **training** can be integrated into
[OpenBridge](https://www.openbridge.no/) interfaces, rather than sitting beside them as a separate manual, course or
simulator session.

## Why it matters

<div className="my-6 flex flex-col gap-6 md:flex-row md:items-start">
<div className="md:flex-1 [&>p:first-child]:mt-0">

OpenBridge is built for complex industrial systems. From maritime bridges to the food industry,
highly critical environments gain from a design system that makes the interfaces consistent and safe.
One way of ensuring this safety, that OpenBridge is yet to explore, lies in how it is taught.

Today, we see that the teaching of complex systems is usually completely detached from the environment
where they live. It is often communicated through physical manuals, classroom learning or online
certifications. That, quite physical, gap is the foundation for this project.

## What is training?

I am exploring how high complexity work environments can be taught to the users through the systems themselves.
One of the things I am trying to find out through this project, is how to talk about it in shorter words. What
does that long sentence boil down to? Through my process I have tried out several words to describe it:
training, learning, onboarding, practice, help, support and many more combinations of these. I have not yet
landed on one, so for now I use training as an umbrella term.

## How the site is organised

- **Process**: dated entries as the work happens. Insights, ideas,
  decisions and the reasoning behind them.
- **Research**: an annotated library of papers, references and visual inspiration, each
  with key findings and a note on why it is relevant to the project
- **Prototypes**: working prototypes of training inside interfaces, built with the OpenBridge
  components so they can be tried out directly in the browser

## Status

The project is ongoing. Updates are published continuously,and may be
contradicted by later ones. That way the whole process, also dead ends, is documented.
