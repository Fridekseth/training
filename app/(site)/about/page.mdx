# About this project

This project explores how documentation, learning and training can be integrated into
[OpenBridge](https://www.openbridge.no/) interfaces, rather than sitting beside them as a separate manual, course or
simulator session. By doing so we hope that we can make training more accessible and workplaces safer!

## Why it matters

<div className="my-6 flex flex-col gap-6 md:flex-row md:items-start">
<div className="md:flex-1 [&>p:first-child]:mt-0">

OpenBridge is built for complex industrial systems. From maritime bridges to the food industry,
highly critical environments gain from a design system that makes the interfaces consistent and safe.
One way of ensuring this safety, that OpenBridge is yet to explore, lies in how it is taught.

Today, we see that the teaching of complex systems is usually completely detached from the environment
where they live. It is often communicated through paper manuals, classroom learning or online
certifications. That, quite physical, gap is the foundation for this project.

## Scope

This project was done over the course of one full semester, as part of a self directed course in Interaction design at the Oslo School of Architecture and Design. The goal was to explore how the OpenBridge design system could support learning in complex industrial systems. With limited time, my focus was not to deliver a perfect pattern for learning, ready for implementation, but to do a wide exploration as a foundation for further work on the topic. This initial project was completed in December 2026, but in the case of learning in OpenBridge the work will continue in the research lab, as a new research project on the matter is under works.

## Status

The project is done for now, and this website documents the whole process:

- **[Research](/research):** An annotated library of papers, references and visual inspiration used in the project, each with key findings and a note on its relevance.
- **[Process](/blog):** Dated entries posted during the process, sharing insights, ideas, decisions and the reasoning behind them as they happened.
- **[Demo](/prototype):** Working prototypes from both early stages and finished concepts, built with the OpenBridge web components so they can be tested.
- **[Documentation](/documentation):** Finished documentation for the first Learning in OpenBridge concept, covering visual language, UI components and interaction patterns.
- **Report:** A final report, describing the process and learnings and outcome in detail.

This website was built alongside the project, and was used both as a tool to keep track of the process for my own sake, and for documenting and sharing results at the end. If you have questions or feedback, don't hesitate to contact me!
