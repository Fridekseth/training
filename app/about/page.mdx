# About this project

This site documents a self-programmed course in interaction design. The
question I am working on is how **training** can be integrated into
[OpenBridge](https://www.openbridge.no/) interfaces — the design system for
ship bridges — rather than sitting beside them as a separate manual, course or
simulator session.

## Why it matters

Bridge systems are learned under pressure, often on the job, across equipment
from different vendors. A design system can make those interfaces consistent;
it does not by itself make them teachable. That gap is what I want to explore.

## How the site is organised

- **Process** — dated entries as the work happens: sketches, dead ends,
  decisions and the reasoning behind them.
- **Research** — an annotated library of papers, standards and references, each
  with key findings and a note on why it matters here.

## Status

Ongoing. Entries are published as the course runs, so earlier posts may be
contradicted by later ones — that is the point of keeping the log.
